Contract: brand-contract v1
# Channels
## product-ui
Terse operational register. One verb per action. Errors name the next action, without raw provider response or secret. Forbidden: jokes in errors/destructive confirm; unsupported verification/safety claims.

## github-readme
Operator-brief. State the job first, then download links and a current screenshot with an explicit demo-data caption. Keep setup, boundaries and verification in linked sections. No unmeasured performance or live-provider claims.

## github-about
One concrete sentence naming supported products, local account management and delivery platforms. No release status or version that can become stale.

## release-notes
Name observable changes and the action needed after updating. State signing limitations and distinguish published releases from unpublished candidates.

## product-wiki
Operational reference with a version, date and source receipt. Distinguish implementation, published binaries and live-provider acceptance.
