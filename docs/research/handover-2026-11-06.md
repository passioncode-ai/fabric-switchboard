# Handing an account over to another machine or a colleague (SB-283, SB-284)

Operator, 2026-11-06 (chat of this session and fabric-workspace d53abe38, relayed by studio-agent
on the board, message `d3848fb7`): see on each account where it works (machine, macOS user,
organization, group); manage accounts from the main MacBook; hand an organization's account over to
the Switchboard of a colleague who connected to the organization; do not tie an account to an
organization by its e-mail domain alone.

## What the Studio move did by hand (studio-agent DEC-0020, DEC-0021)

1. `switchboard settings export` on the MacBook, `settings import --apply` in each Studio space:
   groups and folders, never a credential.
2. In the target space `switchboard login begin --headless`, the code from claude.ai into
   `login code`, then `login finish`.
3. On the MacBook `switchboard accounts update <id> --elsewhere true`.

A credential is never copied: a renewal on one machine ends the other's sign-in
([two-machines-2026-10-23](two-machines-2026-10-23.md) D-2).

## Proposal

- **A handover record (SB-283).** `switchboard accounts hand-over <id> --to <name>` on the managing
  machine writes `switchboard-handover/1`: provider, the account's address and provider identity,
  the group and organizations it serves there, the recipient's name, when; signed with the
  settings signing key (SB-272), no credential. The account is marked `elsewhere` with
  `where = <name>` (SB-284) in the same step.
- **Receiving (SB-283).** `switchboard accounts receive <file> --signer <key>` on the recipient's
  Switchboard checks the signature, shows what will happen, and starts the sign-in for that
  address in the named group (headless when asked). The record names the group by its settings
  file pool, so a colleague who imported the organization's settings file has it.
- **The sign-in code.** The address's mail arrives in the organization's Fabric Inbox, not at the
  colleague. Owner of the channel: Inbox (Workspace `11a5fd08`, Vesta `6fa8228c`); until Inbox
  offers a one-address, time-boxed grant, the administrator relays the code by hand.
- **The record of who holds it.** Vesta's provider accounts (SOT-04, table `provider_accounts`,
  not built yet) will hold owner = the organization or a named member; Switchboard writes its
  handover there once that exists (Vesta `f7e06d56`).
- **Which accounts.** Until the operator decides, only Team or Enterprise seats and API keys may be
  handed to another person (Workspace `11a5fd08`: a personal Claude or ChatGPT subscription may be
  the person's alone under the provider's terms); the operator's own machines are any account.
- **Where each account works (SB-284).** `elsewhere` gains `where` (machine and macOS user), set by
  hand or by a handover; Accounts gets the filter *This machine · Other machines*; CLI and MCP
  carry it.
- **Domain as a hint (SB-285).** The e-mail domain rule stops placing an account in an
  organization by itself; it suggests, and the person or the administrator confirms. Accounts
  placed by the domain today keep their organization (written as the person's word at the
  migration), so nothing running changes.
